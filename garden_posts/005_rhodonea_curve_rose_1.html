I first came across the concept of a rhodonea curve when I was trying to figure out how to draw flowers in p5.js. Imagine a drawing line around a circle, joining together points, where the position of each point is determined by its distance from the center. The point moves around a circle while its distance from the center goes up and down like a wave, you’ll get petals. Incidentally, a rhodonea curve is also called a rose!
